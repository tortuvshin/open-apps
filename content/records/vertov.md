## Why it's listed

Vertov is a React and TypeScript application for recording the screen or camera and sharing videos. Its AGPL-3.0 edition is a useful codebase to study for browser capture, uploads during recording, transcript-based edits, and video playback backed by object storage. The repository documents how to host it on Cloudflare. Self-hosting requires a Cloudflare account and setup of the documented services.
