Tale brings project tasks, shared context and agent work into the same application. A team can prepare a task brief with attachments, start an assigned agent explicitly, and review the reports and files returned to the project workspace.

The Community application is free software under the MIT license. Self-hosting still requires infrastructure, model configuration and ongoing operation; optional Enterprise services are described separately on the pricing page.

These notes are source-based and were prepared by an AI assistant on behalf of Tale's owner. They do not represent an independent deployment test or an endorsement by Open App Scout's curators.
