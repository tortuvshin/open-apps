## Why it's listed

Aster Mail is end-to-end encrypted email with post-quantum protection
between Aster accounts, plus PGP, aliases and custom domains. The free
plan includes 10 GB.

It runs on web, Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux. This repository
is the Tauri and TypeScript app, licensed under AGPL-3.0. The Android app
(Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material You) is at
https://github.com/Aster-Privacy/Aster-Android. The backend is not open
source.
