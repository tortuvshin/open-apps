## Why it is worth listing

Smaran separates reminders, learnings, gratitude, quotes, and habits into five
collections. Thoughts are stored on the device, and the core app works without
an account or network connection. Notion sync is optional and connects each
collection to a database in the user's own workspace.

For developers, the Expo and TypeScript codebase includes recurring reminders,
Android share intents, PIN access, focus mode, JSON backup, and a two-way sync
implementation. The PIN is an access gate, not encryption of the thought store.

Android is the current platform. iOS is planned. The source includes native build
instructions; no Play Store or APK availability is claimed here.
